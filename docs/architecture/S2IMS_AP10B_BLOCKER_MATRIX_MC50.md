## Blocker Descriptions

### 1. Governance Owner Assignment
**Description:** Formal identification of individuals or roles authorized to own AP-10B governance decisions.

**Current Status:** Active / Unresolved — No owner has been formally identified or documented.

**Required Future Evidence:**
- Owner identification document with names, roles, and contact information
- Role definitions specifying governance responsibilities
- Authority mapping showing decision-making boundaries

**Owner Status:** Not assigned

**Approval Status:** Not collected

**Forbidden Shortcut:** Do not claim "owner identified" without formal documentation and stakeholder acknowledgment.
