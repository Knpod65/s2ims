## IMPORTANT NOTICE

**This agenda does NOT assign governance owners.**  
**This agenda does NOT constitute approval, sign-off, or authorization.**  
**No governance checklist items are completed by holding this workshop.**  
**Running this workshop does NOT open AP-10B, AP-10C, or AP-11.**

This is a planning instrument for organizing the governance owner designation process. Holding the workshop is a precondition for, not a replacement of, formal owner assignment.

---

## Workshop Purpose

Bring together the right people to make decisions about who will own each governance role required for AP-10B (and subsequently AP-10C, AP-11) activation. By the end of the workshop, each role should have a named candidate willing to formally accept responsibilities.

## Workshop Agenda

**Total duration**: 90 minutes (can be shortened to 60 minutes if attendees are pre-briefed)

---

### Item 1: Introduction and Context (10 minutes)

**Facilitator**: Project Lead

Topics:
- What S²IMS is and what it does (2 minutes — refer to executive status brief)
- Why governance owners are needed before live data handling (3 minutes)
- What AP-10B means: authorizing the system to import real scholarship data (2 minutes)
- What happens after this workshop (3 minutes)

**Key statement to read aloud**:
> *"This workshop is not a decision-making meeting for system activation. We are here to decide who will take ownership of the governance process. The actual activation will happen later, after each owner has completed their section of the governance checklist."*

---

### Item 2: Role Overview — What Each Owner Must Do (15 minutes)

**Facilitator**: Project Lead

Walk through each of the 5 governance roles. For each:
- Read the role purpose (1 minute)
- State the required authority level (30 seconds)
- List the evidence the owner must produce (1 minute)
- Ask if any attendee has questions about the role (30 seconds)

Roles to cover:
1. AP-10B Authority — final go/no-go decision owner
2. PDPA/Privacy Reviewer — signs PDPA impact assessment
3. Technical Owner — confirms infrastructure readiness
4. Data Owner — defines and signs data contract
5. Decision Chair — convenes and records go/no-go meeting

*(Reference: `docs/governance/S2IMS_GOVERNANCE_OWNER_ASSIGNMENT_PREP_MC74.md`)*

---

### Item 3: Per-Role Discussion (30 minutes — 6 minutes per role)

**Facilitator**: Project Lead

For each role, discuss:

| Question | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Who in this room has the authority required for this role? | Identify candidates |
| Is that person willing to accept this responsibility? | Confirm acceptance |
| Are there any prerequisites this person needs to meet first? | Identify blockers |
| What is the expected timeline for completing this role's evidence? | Set expectations |

**Roles to discuss** (6 minutes each):
1. AP-10B Authority
2. PDPA/Privacy Reviewer
3. Technical Owner
4. Data Owner
5. Decision Chair

---

### Item 4: Evidence Requirements Review (15 minutes)

**Facilitator**: Project Lead

Walk through the 8 evidence items required before AP-10B can open:

| # | Evidence Item | Who Produces It | Timeline Discussion |
|---|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| 1 | PDPA impact assessment (signed) | PDPA Reviewer | [ ] |
| 2 | Data retention policy (written) | PDPA Reviewer + Data Owner | [ ] |
| 3 | Data contract (signed by source + destination) | Data Owner | [ ] |
| 4 | Technical readiness report (code review + infra) | Technical Owner | [ ] |
| 5 | Rollback runbook (tested) | Technical Owner | [ ] |
| 6 | Audit log persistence (verified working) | Technical Owner | [ ] |
| 7 | Import operator training record | Decision Chair | [ ] |
| 8 | Go/no-go decision record (signed) | AP-10B Authority + Decision Chair | [ ] |

*Each owner notes their expected timeline for producing their evidence.*

---

### Item 5: Questions, Blockers, and Timeline (10 minutes)

**Facilitator**: Project Lead

- Are there any role assignments that cannot be made today? (Document the blocker)
- Are there any prerequisites that must be met before someone accepts a role?
- Provisional timeline for completing governance checklist: ___________
- Provisional date for go/no-go meeting: ___________
- Next steps assigned to: ___________

## Questions to Answer Per Role

### AP-10B Authority
- [ ] Do you have direct reporting authority over scholarship data operations?
- [ ] Are you willing to accept risk ownership for live data import decisions?
- [ ] What is your expected availability for the go/no-go meeting?

### PDPA/Privacy Reviewer
- [ ] Do you hold a current PDPA training certification?
- [ ] Do you have a DPO appointment document?
- [ ] What is your expected timeline for completing a PDPA impact assessment?

### Technical Owner
- [ ] Do you have access to the production infrastructure environment?
- [ ] Do you have authority to provision the production database?
- [ ] What is your timeline for completing a technical readiness report?

### Data Owner
- [ ] Do you have authority to define the data contract for scholarship records?
- [ ] Do you have access to the source system schema documentation?
- [ ] What is your timeline for signing off on the allowed fields list?

### Decision Chair
- [ ] Do you have authority to convene cross-functional governance meetings?
- [ ] Do you have calendar authority to schedule the go/no-go meeting?
- [ ] Are you able to document and distribute the go/no-go decision?
